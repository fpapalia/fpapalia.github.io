About My Practice

I believe in a proactive approach to cardiovascular health, focusing on long-term wellness rather than reactive treatment. My London-based cardiology practice is dedicated to the prevention and treatment of heart disease and the comprehensive assessment of cardiometabolic risk factors.

I bring many years of experience in managing complex heart conditions, including looking after those with the most severe heart failure diagnoses, to provide you with a personalised care plan that addresses the interconnected nature of all factors that might be affecting your heart health.

Comprehensive Cardiac Care

I offer holistic cardiovascular assessment and treatment focussed on what matters to you. Some of the factors I take into account when assessing your cardiovascular health are outlined below. Not all of these apply to everyone, and part of my job is to guide you on your journey to a healthy heart.

Heart Disease Prevention

Comprehensive cardiovascular risk assessment and personalized prevention strategies

Weight Management

There are now many evidence-based, highly effective weight loss interventions which can be tailored to optimise cardiovascular health

Sleep Apnoea

Do you snore? Have people mentioned you stop breathing in your sleep? Sleep disordered breathing often goes unrecognised and can have a profound effect on your heart

Renal Disease

Kidney disease often precedes heart disease and can increase your cardiovascular risk. Identifying this early and acting promptly is key to maintaining your heart healthy over the long term

Diabetes

Identification and management of diabetes is an absolutely key focus of cardiovascular protection

Blood pressure Management

Comprehensive hypertension advice and management strategies to achieve optimal control is crucial to reducing the risk of heart problems, as well as stroke and dementia
